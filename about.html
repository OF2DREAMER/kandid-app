PRODUCT & MISSION

About Kandid

Kandid is developed by SolvarionX · Official Domain: kindid.in

01. What is Kandid?

Kandid is an authentic community platform designed around real-world places, university campuses, and genuine everyday moments. Rather than curating an idealized online persona, Kandid provides a grounded space where students, creators, and community members can capture their surroundings honestly and stay connected with the spaces they care about.

02. Why Kandid Exists

Modern social networks frequently incentivize performance over presence. Algorithms prioritize outrage, vanity metrics dictate self-worth, and feeds become saturated with staged content disconnected from physical reality.

Kandid was built on a different premise: real people, real places, real moments.

“Kandid isn't about making your life look interesting. It's about noticing the life you're already living.”

By removing public popularity contests and focusing on shared spatial and campus contexts, Kandid helps communities stay anchored in real-world shared experiences.

03. How It Works

Kandid provides focused, intentional features designed for authentic connection:

📷 Dual-Camera Moment Capture

Capture simultaneous front and rear perspectives in a single moment, showcasing both what you see and your genuine presence within that environment.

🏛️ Campus & Place Hubs

Explore and participate in dedicated communities anchored to real universities, colleges, and shared creative spaces.

🤝 RealMoji Reactions

React to friends and shared community posts using authentic emoji expressions and personal photo reactions rather than passive like counts.

💬 Direct Messaging

Communicate directly with friends and connections in private, focused conversation channels without public broadcast pressures.

04. What Kandid is NOT

  • Not an influencer platform: We do not offer public follower rankings, viral scoreboards, or engagement-maximizing algorithmic feeds designed to induce endless scrolling.
  • Not a broadcast megaphone: Content is rooted in shared community spaces, campuses, and direct friend circles rather than global sensationalism.
  • Not an ad-tracking apparatus: We do not sell user data to advertising brokers or build behavioral profiling dossiers.

05. Privacy & Data Principles

As detailed in our Privacy Policy, our engineering practices are designed to respect personal boundaries:

  • Coarse Context: Moments use campus and city tags rather than continuous background GPS harvesting.
  • No Address Book Syncing: We do not upload or store your device contact book.
  • Account Control: Users retain ownership of their posts and can manage their profile visibility, connections, and data.
